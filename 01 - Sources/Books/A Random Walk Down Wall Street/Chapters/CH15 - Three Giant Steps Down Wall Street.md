## Summary

The book's concluding practical synthesis, presenting three approaches in order of recommendation: the 'no-brainer step' of investing in low-cost, broad-based (increasingly, total-market) index funds; the 'do-it-yourself step' of individual stock-picking under four disciplined rules (buy companies with sustainable above-average growth prospects; never overpay relative to a firm foundation of value; prefer stocks with a plausible 'castle in the air' growth story; trade as little as possible); and the 'substitute-player step' of hiring a professional adviser, with guidance on selecting one. Restates the book's central conclusion that low-cost, broad-based indexing is the most reliable strategy for the overwhelming majority of investors.

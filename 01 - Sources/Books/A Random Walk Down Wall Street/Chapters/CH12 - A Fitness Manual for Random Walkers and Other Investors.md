## Summary

A practical checklist ('exercises') for individual investors: build cash reserves and insurance, keep cash yields competitive with inflation (money-market funds, CDs, Treasury bills), use tax-advantaged accounts (IRAs, Roth IRAs, 529 plans), understand personal investment objectives, prefer home ownership over renting where appropriate, use bonds (including zero-coupon and inflation-indexed TIPS) appropriately, be selective about gold/collectibles, minimize investment costs, and diversify broadly. Primarily actionable personal-finance guidance rather than new quantitative-trading theory.

## Summary

Develops a framework for projecting long-run stock and bond returns from their structural components (dividend yield + earnings growth rate +/- change in valuation multiple, for stocks; current yield for bonds), reviewed across four historical eras of U.S. market returns (Age of Comfort, Age of Angst, Age of Exuberance, Age of Disenchantment) through 2014.

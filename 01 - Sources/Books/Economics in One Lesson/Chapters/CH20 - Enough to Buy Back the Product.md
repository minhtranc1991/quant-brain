## Summary

Hazlitt rebuts underconsumptionist arguments that wages must always be high enough for workers to "buy back" everything they produce, or economic crisis follows, arguing this confuses wages with total purchasing power (which also includes profits, rents, and interest as income later spent or invested) and that the theory does not explain observed cycles of boom and bust.

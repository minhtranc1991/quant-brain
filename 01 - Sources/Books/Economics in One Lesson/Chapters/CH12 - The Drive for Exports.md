## Summary

Hazlitt argues that exports are not an end in themselves; their real economic purpose is to pay for imports. A policy that maximizes exports while minimizing imports (e.g. via subsidy) sacrifices the domestic standard of living improvement that imports would otherwise provide, for the sake of a trade statistic.

## Summary

Hazlitt argues against policies that shorten working hours or spread a fixed amount of work across more workers (e.g. mandated shorter workweeks) on the theory that this creates more jobs, contending that output and total wages fall together with hours unless offset by productivity gains, so the policy does not create net new wealth.

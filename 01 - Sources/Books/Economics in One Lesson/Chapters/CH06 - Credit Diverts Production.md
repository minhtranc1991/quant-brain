## Summary

Hazlitt critiques government-subsidized or guaranteed credit programs (e.g. farm credit, small business loan guarantees) on the grounds that they direct capital toward borrowers a private lender judged too risky, diverting capital away from the uses a market-based risk/return assessment would otherwise have funded, and expose taxpayers to the resulting default risk.

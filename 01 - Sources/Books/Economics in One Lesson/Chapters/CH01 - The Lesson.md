## Summary

Hazlitt states the book's single organizing thesis: good economics consists of tracing the effects of a policy or act not merely for one group but for all groups, and not merely in the immediate term but over the long run. Most economic fallacies come from focusing only on a policy's seen, immediate, single-group effects while ignoring its unseen, delayed, or diffused effects on everyone else.

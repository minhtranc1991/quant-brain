# Chapter 02 — The Broken Window

## Summary

Hazlitt's canonical illustration (from Bastiat): a vandal breaks a baker's window. The crowd reasons that the resulting glazier's business is a net economic gain ("good for the economy"). Hazlitt shows this ignores the unseen alternative: the $50 the baker would otherwise have spent on a new suit. The glazier's gain is exactly offset by the tailor's loss — there is no net new employment, only a diversion of spending, plus the destruction of a window that must now be replaced merely to return to the starting point.

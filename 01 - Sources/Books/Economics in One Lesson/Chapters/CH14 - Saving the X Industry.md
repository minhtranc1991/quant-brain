## Summary

Hazlitt argues that government subsidies or protections aimed at preserving a specific declining industry preserve visible jobs in that industry at the unseen cost of the more productive uses the same capital and labor would otherwise have moved to, slowing the economy's overall adjustment toward higher-value production.

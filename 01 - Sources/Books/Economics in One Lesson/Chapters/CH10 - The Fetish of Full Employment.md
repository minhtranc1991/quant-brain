## Summary

Hazlitt argues that "full employment" is the wrong policy target in itself — maximizing employment without regard to what is produced (e.g. makework) is not the same as maximizing production and living standards; the correct goal is maximum production, of which full employment is normally, but not definitionally, a byproduct.

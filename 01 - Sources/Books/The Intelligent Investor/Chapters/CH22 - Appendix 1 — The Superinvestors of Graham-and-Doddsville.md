## Summary

Warren Buffett's 1984 Columbia essay presents the track records of nine real investors who independently applied Graham-and-Dodd value-investing principles and collectively, persistently beat the market over long periods — offered as a direct empirical rebuttal to the strong claim that no investor can systematically beat the market.

## Summary

Explains how ease of processing (repetition, clear font, primed context, good mood) increases a statement's judged truthfulness and a claim's judged validity ('cognitive ease'), independent of its actual truth — the mechanism behind the illusory-truth effect and behind why familiar-sounding stock names/tickers are traded differently from unfamiliar ones.

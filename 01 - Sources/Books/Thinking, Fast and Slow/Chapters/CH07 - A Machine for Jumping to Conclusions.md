## Summary

Introduces WYSIATI ('What You See Is All There Is') — System 1 constructs the most coherent possible story from currently available information without allowing for missing evidence, and is largely insensitive to both the quality and the quantity of that information.

Text: System 1 operates on a 'What You See Is All There Is' (WYSIATI) principle: it constructs the most coherent possible story from currently available information, suppressing ambiguity and rarely factoring in evidence it does not have — a mechanism that produces overconfident judgments precisely because the felt confidence of a conclusion depends on the coherence of the story that can be told, not on the amount or quality of evidence the story is based on.

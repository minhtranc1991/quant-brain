# Chapter 13 — Availability, Emotion, and Risk

## Summary

Extends the availability heuristic with the 'affect heuristic' (Slovic): people's risk judgments are driven by how they feel about an activity/technology, not primarily by systematic evidence, and public/media availability cascades (a vivid incident triggering intense, self-reinforcing media and public attention) can distort collective risk-perception and policy response far out of proportion to actual statistical risk.

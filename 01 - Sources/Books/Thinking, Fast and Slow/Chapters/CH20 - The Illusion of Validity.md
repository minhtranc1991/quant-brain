## Summary

Kahneman's own account of consulting for a major investment firm: individual stock pickers' year-to-year performance correlated near zero with genuine skill (consistent with a game of chance) despite the firm rewarding and promoting based on those results, and traders/analysts nonetheless felt, and were treated as having, genuine skill — an 'illusion of validity' that persists even when its statistical falsity is demonstrated directly to the people holding it.

## Summary

Kahneman introduces the book's two-systems vocabulary (System 1: fast, automatic, intuitive; System 2: slow, effortful, deliberate), explains the book's purpose (giving readers a richer, more precise language for discussing judgment and choice errors), and previews the three parts covering heuristics/biases, overconfidence, and choice under risk.

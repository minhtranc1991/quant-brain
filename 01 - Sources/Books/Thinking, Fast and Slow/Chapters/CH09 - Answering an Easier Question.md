## Summary

Introduces the substitution heuristic: when faced with a hard question (e.g. 'how happy are you with your life these days'), System 1 often substitutes and answers an easier, related question (e.g. 'what is my mood right now') without noticing the substitution — the general mechanism underlying representativeness and several other heuristics.

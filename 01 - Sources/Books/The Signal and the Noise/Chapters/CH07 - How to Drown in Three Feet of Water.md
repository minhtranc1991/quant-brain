## Summary

Examines macroeconomic forecasting, arguing economic data is inherently noisy (frequently revised after initial release), the economy is a highly dynamic, reflexive system, and professional forecasters exhibit systematic herding and status-quo bias - clustering their forecasts near consensus and near recent trend rather than risking a distinctive, potentially embarrassing call, which is individually rational (career risk) but collectively uninformative.

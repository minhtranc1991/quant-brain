## Summary

Examines earthquake prediction, a field with a long history of confident but failed forecasts (including pseudoscientific approaches). Argues seismologists can reliably estimate long-run base rates (e.g. via the Gutenberg-Richter power-law relationship between earthquake magnitude and frequency) but cannot predict the specific timing of an individual earthquake, a proposed structural limit rather than a temporary data/technique gap.

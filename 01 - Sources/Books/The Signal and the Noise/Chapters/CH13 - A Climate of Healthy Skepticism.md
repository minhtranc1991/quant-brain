## Summary

Examines climate-change forecasting: distinguishes well-established physical mechanisms (the greenhouse effect) from genuinely uncertain quantitative forecasts (climate sensitivity, regional impacts), and critiques both denialist arguments and some published climate forecasts for overfitting short-run noisy temperature data or overstating forecast precision, arguing scientific consensus on the core mechanism should not be conflated with false precision on every downstream quantitative forecast.

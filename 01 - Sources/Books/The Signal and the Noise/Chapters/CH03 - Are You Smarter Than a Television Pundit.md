## Summary

Examines political forecasting failures via Philip Tetlock's 15+ year study of expert political judgment (Expert Political Judgment, 2005): experts performed barely better than chance and worse than simple statistical baselines, and media prominence was inversely correlated with forecast accuracy. Tetlock's key finding is a cognitive-style distinction between "hedgehogs" (Big Idea thinkers, ideologically committed, resistant to revising their frame) and "foxes" (multidisciplinary, adaptive, self-critical, tolerant of complexity) - foxes forecast substantially better.

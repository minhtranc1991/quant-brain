## Summary

Silver frames the book's central thesis: information is exploding, but our ability to extract genuine understanding from it is not - most of the growth in data is noise, not signal. He traces this pattern historically (the printing press produced both scientific progress and centuries of holy war) and argues that better prediction requires a fundamental change in analytical attitude, embodied by Bayes's theorem (introduced in Chapter 8). The book is organized as failures of prediction (Ch. 1-7) followed by successes and Bayesian solutions (Ch. 8-13).

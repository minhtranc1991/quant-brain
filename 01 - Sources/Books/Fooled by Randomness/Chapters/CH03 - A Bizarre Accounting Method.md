## Summary

Introduces 'alternative histories': a decision or track record must be judged against the full range of counterfactual outcomes it could have produced (illustrated with a Russian-roulette thought experiment), not against the single outcome that happened to occur — money earned via a high-risk path is not equivalent to the same money earned via a low-risk path, even though a standard accounting would record them identically.

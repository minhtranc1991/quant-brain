## Summary

Uses Monte Carlo simulation as a metaphor for generating many alternative histories, and a 'dentist' thought experiment (a trader with a 15% expected excess return and 10% annual volatility) to show that the ratio of pleasurable to painful observed moments in a track record depends heavily on how often it is observed, not just on the underlying strategy's true risk/return profile.

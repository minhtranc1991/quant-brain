## Summary

Extends the survivorship/cohort argument with a concrete numeric illustration: of a large initial cohort of speculators, only a small percentage will show a run of profitable years by chance alone, and this fraction is mechanically determined by the size of the initial population and the number of years observed, not by skill; recall also that ergodicity implies these lucky survivors are expected to fare no better than the initial cohort's failures in any future period.

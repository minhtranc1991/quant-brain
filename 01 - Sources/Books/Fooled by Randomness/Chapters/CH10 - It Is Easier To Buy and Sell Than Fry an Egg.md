## Summary

Argues that market-making/trading proficiency observable in a track record can be acquired through sheer repetition without conferring any deeper predictive edge over the market — 'buying and selling' is easier to fake as competence than a genuine, hard-to-fake engineering-grade skill (like frying an egg well).

## Summary

Before building any visualization, establish context: distinguish exploratory analysis (searching the data for what might be noteworthy) from explanatory analysis (communicating one specific, already-identified point to a specific audience). Identify who the audience is, what you need them to know or do, and how you will communicate (live presentation vs. written document), then storyboard the communication before building it.

## Summary

Applies general design concepts — affordances (visual cues for how to interact with something), accessibility (not overcomplicating, using text to label/explain), and the aesthetics-usability effect (more aesthetically pleasing designs are perceived as easier to use and given more benefit of the doubt) — to data visualization, and closes with strategies for gaining audience acceptance of a redesigned visual (articulate benefits, show before/after, offer options, get a vocal audience member on board).

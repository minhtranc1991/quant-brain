# Chapter 05 — The Big Cycles of the Dutch and British Empires and Their Currencies

Source: [[Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order]]

## Summary

Applies the reserve-currency-transition pattern from Chapter 3 to two concrete historical cases. The Dutch guilder's collapse was fast (well under a decade), coinciding with the Netherlands losing a major war (the Fourth Anglo-Dutch War) and being invaded on the continent. The British pound's decline was slower and took two separate devaluation episodes (suspended convertibility in 1947 -> devaluation in 1949; a second devaluation in 1967) before it fully lost reserve-currency status, with UK-held reserves underperforming US assets over the intervening decades even while some holders kept reserves in pounds for political reasons.

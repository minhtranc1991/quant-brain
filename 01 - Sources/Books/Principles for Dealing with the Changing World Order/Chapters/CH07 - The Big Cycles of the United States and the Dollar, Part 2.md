# Chapter 07 — The Big Cycles of the United States and the Dollar, Part 2

## Summary

Traces the post-1945 US dollar-centered world order: the Bretton Woods monetary system, its breakdown in 1971 (Nixon ending dollar-gold convertibility), the inflationary 1970s, the 1979-82 tightening, the disinflationary 1980s, and the debt-financed booms from 1990 through 2020. Presents the 1971 Bretton Woods breakdown as a concrete modern instance of a reserve-currency country moving off a hard-money link under debt/inflation pressure.

## Summary

Dalio states his approach and motivation: an 18-month study of the rise and decline of empires, reserve currencies, and markets, undertaken because he believes the period ahead will be radically different from recent lived experience but similar to earlier historical eras. He frames the whole book around three interrelated big cycles: 1) the long-term money and debt cycle, 2) the domestic wealth and power cycle, and 3) the international wealth and power cycle, and explicitly flags the limits of his own knowledge.

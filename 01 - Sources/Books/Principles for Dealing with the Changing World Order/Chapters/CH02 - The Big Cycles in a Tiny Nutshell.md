## Summary

Dalio previews the book's central claim in compressed form: wealth and power shift between countries over time, following a long-term uptrend (driven by rising productivity/human inventiveness) with large cycles around that trend. The biggest cyclical swings are driven by the interaction of the money/debt cycle with domestic and international politics. He introduces measures of a country's wealth and power (education, innovation, competitiveness, output, trade, military strength, financial-center status, reserve-currency status) used throughout the book to track where a country sits in its Big Cycle.
